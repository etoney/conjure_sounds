# Conjure sound library

Sounds made by the Castle Danger Music Company community in **Heresy**, reviewed and approved for use in **Conjure**.

- Make a sound in [Heresy](https://castledangermusic.com/project/heresy/), press **Send to Conjure**, play it in [Conjure](https://castledangermusic.com/project/conjure/), then **Submit** it for review.
- Approved sounds appear in Conjure under **Sound library**.

## Layout

- `index.json`: the list Conjure reads (name, credit, description, file, size, date added).
- `sounds/*.zip`: one Heresy sound each, WAV notes across the keyboard plus `heresy-sound.json`. The WAVs also load into any DAW sampler.

Approvals are committed automatically from castledangermusic.com. Please don't edit `index.json` by hand unless you know what you're doing.

- `instruments/`: public-domain (CC0) sampled instruments for **Heresy** in Castle Danger Studio, from VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition. See [instruments/CREDITS.md](instruments/CREDITS.md).
- `sfx/`: public-domain (CC0) sound effects for the **Graveyard** module in Castle Danger Studio. Sources and credits are in [sfx/CREDITS.md](sfx/CREDITS.md).

See [TERMS.md](TERMS.md) for how the community sounds may be used.

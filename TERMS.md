These terms cover the community sounds in this library (the `sounds/` folder). The sound effects in `sfx/` and the instruments in `instruments/` are public domain (CC0) from other authors and are not covered by these terms; see [sfx/CREDITS.md](sfx/CREDITS.md) and [instruments/CREDITS.md](instruments/CREDITS.md). They are shown to members, who agree to them when they submit a sound from Conjure on [castledangermusic.com](https://castledangermusic.com).

## If you submit a sound

- You made the sound yourself in Heresy. If it is based on someone else's community preset, credit them in the description.
- You keep ownership of your sound, and you can keep using it in your own personal and commercial work, or share it anywhere else.

- Castle Danger Music Company reviews every sound and may decline it, or remove it later, for any reason.
- Because the license can't be withdrawn, a sound stays in the library once it's approved. You can still ask for it to be taken out, and Castle Danger Music Company decides. Copies people already downloaded, and music they already made with it, are not affected either way.

## If you use a sound from the library

- You can use any sound here free, in your own music and projects, including releases you sell.
- Credit is welcome but not required.
- You may not sell or give away the sounds themselves as a sound pack or sample library.

Questions or a removal request: use the contact page on [castledangermusic.com](https://castledangermusic.com).
